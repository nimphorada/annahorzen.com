I Saw Goody Proctor with the Devil

Px Designer · OZ Arts · 2025

This bombastic, electro-pop musical brings a bratty twist to the Salem Witch Trials, sure to make more than one playwright roll over in his grave. Among the danceable bops, audiences are guaranteed to lose themselves in stadium-pop favorites like “Because It Is My Name,” “Good Girl,” and “How You Like Me Now?” performed in the Amms purely irreverent style. Complete with kitschy choreography and large-scale projections, this take-down of the patriarchy turns this infamous story into a feminist triumph. Who’s ready for a #SalemSummer?
